## What it is

`agentsfleet` runs fleets that respond to messages, webhooks, and schedules.
Each fleet follows instructions you review before saving.

## Why it exists

Give repeated work a fleet you can message, inspect, and stop.

## How it behaves

Start with [your first fleet](/quickstart). Describe a task, review the setup, and send a message.

When you need more:

- [Connect an app](/fleets/connectors) for access to another service.
- [Add a webhook](/fleets/webhooks) to start work when an event arrives.
- [Use memory](/memory) to keep useful information between runs.
- [Manage your workspace](/workspaces/managing) to organize fleets and access.

## Limits

Review tools, access, and spending limits before saving.
Setup chats and runs use the workspace owner's balance.

`agentsfleet` is in early access. Public behavior may change before version 2.0.0.
