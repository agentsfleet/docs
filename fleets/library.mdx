## What it is

Plugins were account-owned revisions of fleet setup.

## Why it exists

A published Plugin let you reuse a reviewed setup across fleets. The current workflow keeps a fleet's instructions and follow-up requests in its own chat.

## How it behaves

To start a fleet in the dashboard, open **New fleet**, select a model, and send your first message. The message creates the fleet and starts its first turn.

Use [`agentsfleet fleet create`](/cli/agentsfleet#chat) in the terminal to do the same. Send later instructions in the fleet's chat.

## Limits

The draft and Plugin APIs, the `agentsfleet plugins` and `agentsfleet chat` commands, and the `plugin:write` scope are retired. The error registry keeps their codes for earlier logs.
